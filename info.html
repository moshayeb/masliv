MASLIV — JEWELRY

Designed in Sweden. Made with care. Each piece is a collaboration between minimal form and skilled craftsmanship.

We believe jewelry should be worn, not saved. Scratches are not flaws—they're memory. Patina is not damage—it's time. Every mark tells a story.

Our work is minimal but not cold. The forms are geometric, but the process is human. We leave the hammer marks visible. We let the material speak.
